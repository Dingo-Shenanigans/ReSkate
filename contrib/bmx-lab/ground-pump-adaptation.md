# Ground pump adaptation

The native BMX chassis consumes mapped left-stick Y for a bounded down-pump. Right-stick hop/manual/trick selection and Skate's D-pad retain their existing paths.

This is an independent single-chassis adaptation of the current C1C8 BMX Streets `SimplePumpForce` input/window contract. Nominal authored `.11` VelocityChange at `.01` seconds is represented as **11 m/s² per unit input**, applied at the measured chassis COM in world-down. It does not change native gravity, time scale, body pose or center of mass. The complex Streets PumpSystem and child-body forces are excluded. Runtime Streets overrides and equivalent handling have not been established.

Pump requires an enabled fresh operator and current strict wheel support, including the measured opening-speed and separation checks. Balance continuation and speculative query skin do not qualify. Direct pumping requires a neutral/released left stick after equip, pause, reset, flight, manual or hop interruption. Down input below -.3 can apply for at most .45 seconds; holding cannot restart it. Hop, preload and manual/nose requests inhibit the pump. World upward velocity above .5 m/s rejects the force while consuming its window time.

After an observed supported-to-air-to-supported cycle with more than .15 seconds of measured unsupported intervals, manual and nose values no greater than .05, and no hop/preload inhibition, one automatic -.4 command lasts at most .3 seconds. This automatic window can activate while left-stick Y remains held. The first unsupported observation starts its air clock at zero. Direct and automatic commands use the stronger magnitude rather than summing. Active pedal input scales magnitude by .75. Partial final steps preserve duration integrals across rates. Cold airborne startup, lost ownership, invalid samples, pause or bailout cannot supply landing history.

The force component is inserted into portable velocity prediction **before** current tire contacts are solved. Base impulse accounting retains pump while removing only native gravity and the separately submitted hop. All resulting components use the existing common force/torque budgets. Flat contact therefore gains normal load immediately while its net submitted vertical force still balances native gravity. The implementation never submits a second pump command or repairs position.

Ride trace and native self-test samples expose `pump_force`, `pump_applied`, `pump_status`, `pump_acceleration`, `pump_automatic_armed`, `pump_direct_seconds`, `pump_automatic_remaining` and `pump_air_seconds`. `pump_force` is the pre-contact component included in the solve; it is not the final net force or an independently measured applied native force. Use `command_submitted` and the normal/contact force evidence when assessing it. Duplicate native observations do not advance the pump clock or repeat a pump event.

Portable verification covers time integrals, release rearm, direct/automatic precedence, first-touch and operator gates, invalid/reset behavior, same-step load balance, force/mass accounting, hop exclusion, speculative/continuation rejection, budget rejection and one-time clock commit across provisional recovery solves. Native source-only compilation is separate from a linked DLL, native execution, controller consumption, visual acceptance or a BMX Streets handling match.
